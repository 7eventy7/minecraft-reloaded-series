# Minecraft: Reloaded Series

A series of separate resource packs that each improve one specific part of the game. They all stay close to the vanilla look, so you can use as many or as few as you want without changing the overall feel of Minecraft.

Every pack is standalone. There's no core pack or required dependency, and they don't overlap, so load order between them shouldn't matter.

All of them are available as the Vanilla Reloaded collection on [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/collection/sEiYgNpa) and [Planet Minecraft](https://www.planetminecraft.com/collection/93441/vanilla-reloaded/).

## Installing

The easiest way is to grab the packs you want from Modrinth (on the site or through the Modrinth App) or from Planet Minecraft.

To install manually, download the pack's zip, put it in your `resourcepacks` folder, and enable it from Options > Resource Packs in game.
